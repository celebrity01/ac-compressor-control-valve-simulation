In State 1, the Electronic Control Valve Solenoid is open. High-pressure discharge refrigerant enters the crankcase chamber.

(1) Electronic Control Valve Open (Destroked)

Discharge gas flows into the crankcase. High back-pressure ($P_c \approx P_d$) pushes the swashplate upright ($\alpha \approx 2^\circ-5^\circ$). Minimal piston travel prevents evaporator freeze-up and cuts engine parasitic load.

(2) Electronic Control Valve Closed (Full Stroke)

Discharge port is sealed. Crankcase gas vents to suction ($P_c \approx P_s$). Spring and cylinder forces tilt the swashplate to maximum angle ($\alpha \approx 20^\circ-22^\circ$), delivering 100% displacement for maximum cabin cooling.
